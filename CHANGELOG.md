# Changelog

All notable changes to GeoStamp are listed here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

First release.

### Added
- Camera with live preview, flash and front/back switch; one-time location lookup when a photo is taken.
- Stamp editor: place name, address, coordinates, label, date, time and time zone, with a live preview.
- Two stamp layouts: classic bar and map card (small OpenStreetMap picture, title, address, coordinates, long date, note).
- Saved places with search as you type, group codes and a place name written before the address; places are managed in Settings.
- Pick a place with Google Maps: share a place to GeoStamp, or paste a link or coordinates.
- Gallery with albums, full-screen viewer with zoom, swiping between photos, details, rename, delete, share and export to a folder.
- Import one or many photos with the system photo picker; select several photos to stamp, share, export, move or delete together.
- Photo metadata (EXIF) inspector and an explicit editor that always writes to a new copy.
- Saving always makes a new copy in Pictures/GeoStamp; the original photo stays untouched inside the app.
