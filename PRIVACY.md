# Privacy

GeoStamp has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server of its own.
Photos, locations and saved places stay on your phone.

The app uses the internet only when you ask for something that needs it:

- **Google Maps short links:** reading where a pasted or shared maps.app.goo.gl link points (the link is requested; nothing else is sent).
- **Address lookup:** Android's own address service turns coordinates into an address.
- **Map card layout:** downloading a small OpenStreetMap picture for the place. This tells OpenStreetMap roughly where the place is (to about 300 metres). No photo is sent.

Permissions: camera (to take photos) and location (optional, to stamp where a photo was taken).
A stamp with a typed location is a label, not proof that a photo was taken there.
